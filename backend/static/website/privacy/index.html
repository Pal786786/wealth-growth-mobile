Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026 • Effective: January 2026

1. Who we are

My Funding Bank Private Limited (“MFB”, “we”, “us”) is a fintech platform incorporated in India offering investment, forex (demo), travel and AI-advisory services through the apps and websites at myfundingbank.com, app.myfundingbank.com and admin.myfundingbank.com.

2. Data we collect

We collect (a) identity data — name, mobile, email, PAN, Aadhaar (for KYC); (b) financial data — bank account, transactions, holdings; (c) device & usage data — IP, device ID, OS, app activity; (d) optional data — photos for KYC, profile preferences. We do not collect data about your contacts, calls, SMS or location without explicit, contextual consent.

3. How we use your data

To operate accounts, process transactions, meet KYC/AML obligations, personalise your experience (e.g. AI advisor), prevent fraud, send service notifications, comply with regulators (RBI, SEBI). We never sell data to advertisers.

4. Sharing & disclosure

Only with: (a) regulated financial institutions for executing trades/transfers; (b) KYC/AML providers; (c) cloud infrastructure providers under strict DPAs; (d) regulators when legally compelled. All third parties are contractually bound by data-protection terms.

5. Security

AES-256 encryption at rest, TLS 1.3 in transit, AWS/India region hosting, ISO 27001 controls, annual penetration testing, 24x7 fraud monitoring. PII is masked in internal dashboards.

6. Your rights (DPDP Act 2023)

Access, correct, port or delete your personal data anytime from Settings → Privacy or by emailing privacy@myfundingbank.com. We will action requests within 30 days. You may also withdraw consent for non-essential processing.

7. Cookies & tracking

We use first-party cookies for authentication and analytics. No third-party advertising cookies. You can clear cookies in your browser at any time.

8. Children

Our services are not directed to anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect minor data.

9. Changes

We will notify material changes via app banner and email at least 14 days in advance.

10. Contact

Grievance Officer: privacy@myfundingbank.com • +91 80 1234 5678 • BKC, Mumbai 400051. Response SLA: 15 days.
